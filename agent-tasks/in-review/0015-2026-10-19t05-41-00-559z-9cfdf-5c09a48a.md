## Root cause and fix

Poma tagged several coffees (including `volcancito-honey-caturra`, `san-sebastian-washed-castillo`, `la-divisa-ef2-natural-castillo`) as `b2b-only` in Shopify. Those product pages render `<main><h1>Access denied</h1></main>` for anonymous visitors, so the page has no `application/ld+json` Product script and the webshop-item-processor parser 307 logs `No product data found for <url>` and throws. Verified live: every `b2b-only` product returns an "Access denied" page, every other Coffee product returns a parseable page with ld+json.

Fix: the webshop-processor parser 307 now filters out products tagged `b2b-only` from `/collections/all/products.json` (same pattern as the existing `Kawy` tag filter in parser 314), so the item processor is no longer invoked for wholesale-only pages. Verified with `ROASTER_ID=307` runs of both workspace tests: the three URLs are excluded from discovery and the remaining 7 product pages parse without errors.
